# PET/CT and Molecular Imaging

## Coverage
Oncology PET/CT, amyloid/tau/FDG PET, PET/MR, SPECT/CT, reconstruction, denoising, SUV/SUVR, Centiloid, lesion tracking, motion, theranostics, dosimetry and acquisition QA.

## September 2026 signal
FDA 510(k) K261305 for **MICSI-PET** has a decision date of Sept. 11, 2026. Secondary reporting describes MRI-guided PET enhancement and neuro-PET quantification functions; the FDA record remains the regulatory source of truth.

## OraDigit research question
Where can OraDigit improve quantitative consistency, workflow and longitudinal interpretation without duplicating reconstruction algorithms already offered by established vendors?
