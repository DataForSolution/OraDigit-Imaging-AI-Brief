# Adoption Signals

Track evidence that a technology is moving from demonstration to routine use.

## Stronger signals
Multi-site deployment, prospective clinical evidence, reimbursement, guideline incorporation, measurable workflow improvement, repeat purchasing and deep PACS/RIS/EHR/scanner integration.

## Weaker signals
Funding announcements, conference demos, unverified accuracy claims and regulatory clearance without deployment evidence.

Every weekly brief should label adoption-signal strength explicitly.
