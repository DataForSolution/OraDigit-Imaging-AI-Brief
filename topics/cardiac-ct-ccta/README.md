# Cardiac CT and Coronary CTA (CCTA)

## Coverage
Patient selection/preparation, heart-rate control, motion, contrast timing, acquisition/reconstruction, radiation dose, calcium, stenosis, CAD-RADS, quantitative plaque analysis, CT-derived physiology where applicable, reporting and reimbursement.

## Current reimbursement signal
CMS local coverage policies recognize defined circumstances for **AI-enabled quantitative coronary topography/coronary plaque analysis (AI-QCT/AI-CPA)** after an eligible CCTA is completed and interpreted. Coverage is conditional, not a blanket screening or surveillance benefit.

```text
appropriate patient
→ technically adequate CCTA
→ physician interpretation
→ eligible quantitative post-processing
→ documented medical necessity
→ coding/claim
→ clinical action
```

## OraDigit research direction
A high-value module may focus on protocol readiness, motion/heart-rate risk, acquisition QA, required metadata completeness, decision support for advanced post-processing and structured handoff.
