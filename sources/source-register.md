# Source Register

## Evidence hierarchy

| Tier | Source type | Typical use |
|---|---|---|
| A | FDA, CMS, Federal Register, NIH/NLM, professional societies | Regulatory, coverage, safety and official policy |
| B | Peer-reviewed journals and conference proceedings | Clinical and technical evidence |
| C | Manufacturer regulatory filings and official product documentation | Product capability and intended use |
| D | Reputable imaging/healthcare trade publications | Timely industry context |
| E | Vendor press releases | Company-reported launches, partnerships and deployment claims |

## Handling rules

1. Do not host copyrighted PDFs unless redistribution permission is explicit.
2. Official public PDFs may be linked but are not mirrored by default.
3. Vendor claims must be attributed.
4. Regulatory clearance does not itself establish better outcomes, reimbursement or adoption.
5. Coverage and coding are checked independently from FDA status.
