# OraDigit Imaging AI Brief

A source-backed research and publishing repository for consequential developments in medical imaging, imaging AI, molecular imaging, cardiac imaging, radiology workflow, therapy analytics, clinical decision support, reimbursement, regulation, and adoption.

## Mission

The project converts fast-moving imaging developments into a durable weekly intelligence record for **OraDigit**. The emphasis is not on collecting headlines. Each brief asks:

1. **What changed?**
2. **What is verified?**
3. **Why does it matter clinically or operationally?**
4. **What could it mean for OraDigit products, research, partnerships, or positioning?**
5. **What still needs verification?**

## Coverage

Core areas include:

- PET/CT, PET/MR, SPECT/CT, theranostics, quantitative PET and molecular imaging
- Cardiac CT and coronary CTA (CCTA), coronary plaque analysis, CT-FFR and quantitative cardiac imaging
- CT, MRI, ultrasound, mammography, X-ray and multimodality imaging AI
- radiology workflow, orchestration, triage, reporting and quality improvement
- therapy analytics, radiotherapy planning and image-guided treatment workflows
- clinical decision support, appropriate-use logic and imaging order optimization
- FDA regulation, CMS coverage, coding and reimbursement
- vendor releases, partnerships, deployments and evidence
- adoption signals, implementation barriers, interoperability and governance

## Weekly cadence

A Monday brief normally covers developments since the prior brief. High-impact events may be added between cycles and rolled into the next Monday issue.

Each brief distinguishes:

- **Verified development** — directly supported by a primary or authoritative source.
- **Vendor-reported development** — attributed to the company or press release.
- **Analysis** — OraDigit interpretation of why the development matters.
- **Watch item** — a signal that is relevant but not yet strong enough for a firm conclusion.

## Evidence standard

Preferred sources, in order:

1. FDA, CMS, Federal Register, NIH/NLM, professional societies and other authoritative public bodies
2. peer-reviewed journals and conference publications
3. manufacturer regulatory filings and official product documentation
4. reputable healthcare/imaging trade publications
5. vendor press releases, clearly labeled as vendor claims

No privately purchased or access-controlled PDF is used as automated source text. Copyrighted full-text documents are not copied into this repository. Notes paraphrase source material and link to the lawful source.

## Publication standard

A public-facing note should:

- use original language and analysis;
- cite the underlying source with date and URL;
- avoid long quotations;
- distinguish clearance, approval, coverage and reimbursement;
- avoid implying clinical effectiveness from regulatory clearance alone;
- state uncertainty and evidence limitations;
- avoid patient-specific medical advice.

## Strategic lens for OraDigit

The repository continuously evaluates opportunities in five layers:

1. **Acquisition quality** — protocol optimization, image quality, dose, motion and technologist workflow.
2. **Interpretation intelligence** — detection, segmentation, quantification, longitudinal comparison and structured reporting.
3. **Decision support** — ordering, appropriateness, protocol selection, downstream recommendations and denial prevention.
4. **Therapy analytics** — response assessment, dosimetry, treatment planning and longitudinal outcome measurement.
5. **Operational intelligence** — workflow orchestration, quality improvement, utilization, reimbursement and implementation monitoring.
